# Infeniti × Cuberto — page brief

Goal (user decision, 2026-11-24): rebuild **cuberto.com**'s layout, type scale, motion and
interactions at `/`, filled with **infeniti.io**'s real content and assets, recoloured with the
Infeniti brand tokens.

| Token | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| brand | `#a2cf36` | buttons, links, highlights, cursor, hover states |
| ink | `#191919` | main text, dark sections, footer (replaces Cuberto `#000`) |
| paper | `#f8f8f8` | main background, text on dark (replaces Cuberto `#fff`) |

## Route map

| Source | Local |
|---|---|
| cuberto.com/ (structure, CSS, motion) + infeniti.io/ (content, assets) | `/` → `src/app/page.tsx` |

Out-of-scope links point to `https://www.infeniti.io/...` (about, service, work, career, contact,
privacy-policy, project pages) and Calendly.

## Scale system (from Cuberto CSS)

`html{font-size}`: `2.66667vw` (<768), `.625vw` (≥768), `10px` (≥1600). All sizes in rem.
Container: `0 1.5rem` mobile; `max-width:1600px; padding:0 12rem` desktop, `-size-lg` 24rem.
Smooth scrolling: Lenis (`html.lenis`).

## Section order (Cuberto → Infeniti content)

1. Navbar — logo, Services ▾ (5 service pages), Works, Company, Careers, CTA "Contact Now".
   Absolute at top; after scrolling it becomes fixed, hides on scroll-down, reappears on scroll-up
   with blurred paper fill. Mobile: rounded pill strip, 2-line toggle, expanding menu.
2. TopheadSection — h1 9rem/500/100% centred, `-.01em`; text 2.4rem/120%.
   "Software Built Around Your Business".
3. PreviewSection — 1920/1080 rounded 2rem media card, gradient overlay, header 8rem, label pill.
   Media: Infeniti team photo (`work/hero-showcase.webp`).
4. OverviewSection — caption (uppercase 1.6rem) left 28%, text 2.56rem right. "About Infeniti".
5. FeatureSection — 5 Cuberto cards → 4 Infeniti services. Grey `#eee` cards turn ink with
   scroll; header row title 4.2rem + number `01`; text 2rem/140%; "Explore" more-button.
   Cuberto's illustrations are replaced by brand-coloured SVG art (no Cuberto media reused).
6. LogoreelSection — "Trusted by Leading Clients", 4-col grid, hover dims siblings.
7. SummarySection inverse (ink, radius 8rem, overlaps next by 8rem) — "Our Best Works",
   2 staggered columns (50rem each, right col offset 15rem), 5 projects, "View Works".
8. SummarySection fill — testimonials fan (5 cards rotated; straighten on hover).
9. OverviewSection — "Why Infeniti" + 5 stat tiles (12-col grid, spans 4/4/4/6/6).
10. FaqSection inverse — "What We Offer": 5 service rows with elastic dividers (links, not
    accordions: Infeniti has no FAQ copy).
11. OutroSection — ink, 69.2rem tall, Infeniti `hero-bg.webp` glow, "Have a business problem
    worth solving?", CTA "Explore".
12. Footer — CTA pills, countries, nav grid (2 cols), socials, privacy, copyright.

## Interactions

- Cursor (pointer:fine only): 10rem circle scaled `.1`; `.25` on links; `.8` with arrow icon on
  work cards / logos. Lime fill.
- CtaButton: pill; hover = ripple rising from bottom (`.5s cubic-bezier(.4,0,0,1)`), label rolls
  up (`.8s cubic-bezier(.16,1,.3,1)`), `scaleX(1.02)` springy.
- Nav/footer links: label roll-up with scaled clone.
- Headings: word-by-word rise on entering viewport.
- Divider: 1px SVG line that bends toward the pointer and springs back.

## Deliberate differences

- Font: Infeniti's Inter Display (OFL) instead of Cuberto's commercially licensed Suisse Intl.
- Cuberto media (showreel video, feature illustrations, outro bg) not reused; Infeniti assets or
  brand SVG used instead.
- Work card aspect follows Infeniti's 1600×757 previews (Cuberto uses 500/675 portrait).
- Cuberto's Insights/blog section omitted (Infeniti has no blog); FAQ became a service list.
